02 · Clarify · 13 minutes

Daily Habits: Statements, Questions & Negatives

Let's refresh statements first, then explore how to ask questions and say what someone doesn't do.

🔁 Quick refresh · Present Simple statements

I / You / We / They + base verb → I pack my schoolbag. They practice after school.

He / She / It + verb-s / -es → Leo takes a break. Maya practices football.

For consonant + y, change y to -ies: study → studies. For many verbs ending in -ch, -sh, -ss, -x, or -o, add -es: watch → watches.

❓ Questions

Do you play after school?

Does she practice on Fridays?

Where does your brother go?

Do / Does + subject + base verb?

🚫 Negatives

I don't stay up late.

He doesn't watch TV before homework.

They don't have practice today.

Subject + don't / doesn't + base verb

04 · Reading · 10 minutes

The After-School Switch

Read carefully. Find details, understand new words in context, and make an inference.

Every weekday, eleven-year-old Noah follows a busy schedule. When school finishes, he usually walks home with his friend Oliver. Noah doesn't start playing video games immediately. First, he puts away his schoolbag, has a snack, and checks his homework planner. On Tuesdays and Thursdays, he practices basketball at the community sports center.

Oliver's afternoons look quite different. He doesn't play on a sports team, but he spends time drawing characters for a comic book. He sometimes stays at school for an art club. His parents don't mind that he enjoys quiet activities, as long as he finishes his homework.

On Fridays, the boys meet online to play a racing game. Noah doesn't stay up late because he has basketball practice on Saturday mornings. Oliver usually plays a little longer. Even though they have different interests, they always find time to hang out.
